# References

Works used or built on. Details are from memory; confirm authors, venues and years against the
originals before citing. FinMRAGBench and HierFinRAG come from the project proposal and must be
verified against their original publications.

**Benchmarks**
- Chen et al. *FinQA: A Dataset of Numerical Reasoning over Financial Data.* EMNLP 2021.
- Islam et al. *FinanceBench: A New Benchmark for Financial Question Answering.* 2023.
- Zhu et al. *TAT-QA: A Question Answering Benchmark on a Hybrid of Tabular and Textual Content in Finance.* ACL 2021.
- Chen et al. *ConvFinQA: Exploring the Chain of Numerical Reasoning in Conversational Finance QA.* EMNLP 2022.
- *FinMRAGBench* (ACL 2026) and *HierFinRAG* (2026): as cited in the proposal.

**Reasoning with tools**
- Yao et al. *ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models.* ICLR 2023.
- Chen et al. *Program of Thoughts Prompting.* TMLR 2023.
- Gao et al. *PAL: Program-aided Language Models.* ICML 2023.

**Retrieval**
- Robertson and Zaragoza. *The Probabilistic Relevance Framework: BM25 and Beyond.* 2009.
- Cormack, Clarke and Buettcher. *Reciprocal Rank Fusion outperforms Condorcet and individual rank learning methods.* SIGIR 2009.
- Asai et al. *Self-RAG: Learning to Retrieve, Generate, and Critique through Self-Reflection.* 2023.
- Yan et al. *Corrective Retrieval Augmented Generation (CRAG).* 2024.
- Faysse et al. *ColPali: Efficient Document Retrieval with Vision Language Models.* 2024 (a natural extension of `mmrag`).
